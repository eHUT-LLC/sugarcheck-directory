How sponsorship works

Transparency: Sponsors support SugarCheck, but they do not control health education or clinical recommendations.

Rotation: Proposed weights of 1, 3, and 6 indicate relative selection priority, not a guaranteed percentage of views, clicks, customers, or revenue.

Privacy: Individual users' names, diabetes information, activity, and sponsor interactions are not provided to sponsors. See the app privacy policy for current app data practices.

Annual plans: Proposed annual terms are prepaid for 12 months with a 15% discount and four approved listing edits (one per quarter; unused edits do not roll over). Final cancellation and refund provisions will be presented before any checkout is enabled.
