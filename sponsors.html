How the strip works, exactly

Every time a person opens the Home screen or a carb-check result, the app draws one sponsor at random, weighted by tier, and redraws every 30 seconds while the screen is up. It never shows the same name twice in a row. Over a month, your share of screen time is your weight divided by the sum of all sponsors’ weights that month.

What you will and won’t get

You get: the app’s overall install count from the Google Play Store, monthly. You never get: who opened the app, who saw your name, who tapped it, anyone’s name, diabetes type, location, reminders or saved foods. The app doesn’t collect those; there is nothing to give. That is the promise the app makes its users (privacy policy), and it’s why they trust what it shows them — including your name.
