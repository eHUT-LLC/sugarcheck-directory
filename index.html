SugarCheck by eHUT™ is a free Android app that helps adults managing Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes understand food labels, build everyday habits, and find community care resources.

Designed to be local-first. No account required. Some food and directory lookups use internet connections; learn more in our privacy policy.
