SugarCheck by eHUT™

A free Android app for people managing Type 1 or Type 2 diabetes. It identifies, educates and reminds. Your care team governs.

No ads. No account. Nothing about you leaves your phone.

Scan a labelBarcode or a photo of the Nutrition Facts panel. Carbs, carb choices, added sugar, fiber, sodium — and a plain green / yellow / red band.
Build a plateType what’s on the plate — cornbread, greens, fried catfish — and get the meal total. Half, one or two portions in food words.
Gentle remindersSugar check, meds, water, a walk. Done or Later. Quiet hours. Your first name and nothing else.
Diabetes care near youPharmacies, clinics, dietitians, foot and eye care — hours, open-now, tap for directions.
Sugar glossaryWhat Type 1 and Type 2 are, A1c, carb choices, and 40 names sugar hides under on a label.
Kitchen modeThree big buttons for wet hands and reading glasses on the counter.
